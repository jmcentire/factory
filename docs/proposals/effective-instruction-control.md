# Effective instruction control

> Status: P0 and disposition-bearing consumption implemented 2026-08-19; behavioral
> qualification and the closed advisory launch envelope remain planned. This document grants no authority and does not weaken the
> signed phase-artifact, tool-policy, obligation, broker, or promotion boundaries.

## Research disposition

The useful finding is not that Factory needs another memory system. It is that instruction
storage, instruction delivery, model adherence, and effect enforcement are four different
boundaries.

- Kindex already supplies durable contextual memory. It remains context and evidence, never
  authority, isolation, or a secure projection boundary.
- `AGENTS.md`, `CLAUDE.md`, provider memory, and model summaries are probabilistic context. They
  are excluded from qualified lanes unless exact bytes are deliberately admitted.
- The three signed phase artifacts remain the only product-intent authority. A directive cannot
  amend one; an apparent conflict with one is a blocking specification question.
- Deterministic requirements remain outside the model in signed policy, transition and
  acceptance obligations, the typed broker, isolation, and promotion denial.

The research claims that static instruction files always hurt, that a subagent guarantees
compliance, that hooks are infallible, or that a universal instruction-count cliff exists are not
adopted. Minimal, scoped instructions are useful; they are still not enforcement. `PostToolUse`
cannot undo an effect, prompt-cache economics prove nothing about authority, and benchmark
averages do not replace qualification of the exact models and tasks Factory runs.

One supplied synthesis is excluded entirely from the evidentiary basis: it invented a Web Audio
Meditate codebase and converted statistically nonsignificant context-file results into confident
deltas. Third-party synthesis may suggest a question; it may not establish a repository fact,
numeric premise, product capability, or implementation target. Phase A0 admits those claims only
after checking live source and a primary publication or vendor document. A mixture of true
surrounding context and a fabricated core is treated as contamination, not partial corroboration.

## Pre-P0 composition failures

Factory's components distinguish context from authority, but that distinction does not yet hold
at every composition boundary.

1. `directive.py active` can select from an unverified or absent ledger. The advisory wake turns
   any selection error into an empty projection and can select an ambient ledger different from
   the one covered by external resume configuration.
2. Lane dispatch admits no effective-directive bytes at all. Its current restatement check is a
   substring search for `interpretation_confirmed: true`; unrelated prose can satisfy it.
3. The canonical Validator/Coder/Tester directives are documentation, not an explicitly admitted
   qualified-runner input. A lane receipt therefore cannot prove which role instructions were
   actually presented.
4. The advisory client inherits its launcher's environment and local client configuration. The
   projection receipt proves input bytes but not the executable, argv, environment, or config that
   interpreted them.
5. A blocking advisory event can be cleared by a read receipt alone. That proves attention, not a
   disposition of the finding.

These explain several forms of apparent model disobedience without attributing intent to the
model: the instruction may never have been selected, may not have been delivered, may have been
shadowed by ambient higher-salience context, or may be soft guidance whose violation remains
operationally possible.

## Implementation sequence

### P0 — one effective instruction contract

Before either a lane or advisory model is invoked, derive one closed canonical JSON artifact from
the externally checkpoint-bound directive ledger and provisional side chain. Verify both chains,
resolve explicit supersession and expiry deterministically, retain exact directive ids, scopes,
text, qualifiers, source hashes, chain heads, evaluation time, and verification mode, and refuse
missing, corrupt, oversized, duplicate, or substituted sources. A supersession must point backward
to exactly one extant entry and may have exactly one successor; missing parents, diamonds, cycles,
and partial resolution refuse. Do not infer semantic conflict from prose. Explicit supersession
resolves known conflict; unresolved apparent conflict remains a blocking human/Validator question.

The directive ledger and agent-writable provisional chain do not share an authority class. An
unsettled provisional entry is an attributed candidate, not an operative instruction. Until exact
human authority for its cited ruling is independently verified, its presence blocks dispatch for
ratification/refusal; it cannot silently supersede or augment the founder set merely because its
hash chain is intact.

External checkpoint verification proves which exact ledger bytes the run admitted and the runtime
then proves their hash-chain integrity. It does not independently authenticate the founder or a
hardware signer. The derived contract therefore labels entries as externally checkpoint-bound,
not founder-authenticated. A future signed directive-authority receipt may strengthen that source
class; the current implementation does not manufacture the claim.

The same artifact is admitted as contextual state in both closed profiles and presented in the
prompt. The capsule, retained prompt, and receipt must all bind the same bytes. Any effective-set
change creates a new attempt/checkpoint; it cannot silently alter an existing dispatch.

Replace the substring restatement with a closed operator-supplied JSON dispatch. It must
bind run, generation, role, and exactly the active directive ids, with one
source quote, stated operational consequence, and explicit ambiguity disposition per id. Any
ambiguity blocks. The runtime adds the re-derived effective-set digest to the retained readback;
the caller cannot choose it. A verbatim echo, non-empty field, or matching id proves neither
comprehension nor adherence; the record is exact translation/readback evidence only and carries
no signer or author identity claim. It
must never be exposed as semantic clearance.

### P0 — explicit role instruction delivery

Compile the shared foundation plus exactly one canonical role directive into a bounded,
versioned provider-neutral role contract. Bind its source through the external resume
configuration, admit the compiled bytes as a configuration reference, and put the role contract
in the task prompt's control section. Do not depend on provider auto-discovery, global memories,
skills, or repository instruction files. Record its content digest through the state capsule and
runner receipt.

The effective-directive artifact, readback, and role contract enter one new closed profile
generation as a single logical cutover. Each file uses durable no-replace publication; a crash
between publications is recovered by re-verifying and reusing the exact existing effective
contract, then idempotently completing the remaining files. There is no claim-complete
intermediate mode accepted by the runner. The versioned Factory runtime
owns the schemas and canonical byte serialization; neither directive prose nor phase artifacts
select a schema. A source from an incompatible generation refuses rather than being translated.
The profile digest and all source bytes are bound by the external resume configuration.

Complex procedures should be role- and phase-scoped instead of permanently global. A procedure
that must occur at a fixed lifecycle point becomes an obligation or host action; it is not made
more reliable by adding `MUST` to prose. The canonical role contract should name observable
handoff fields, prohibitions, and self-refutation checks; generic software advice is omitted.

P0 applies fixed pre-model bounds: at most 256 directive entries, 64 qualifiers per entry, 16 KiB
per directive, 64 KiB for each source chain, and 256 KiB for the compiled instruction contract.
Bounds are bytes over the exact UTF-8 source and canonical JSON, not provider token estimates.
All reads use one stable descriptor; the runtime derives once from those admitted bytes and passes
that exact in-memory result to capsule and prompt assembly. The derived view needs no new human
signature because it grants no authority and is re-derived from already bound inputs; substitution
is rejected by exact byte/digest comparison.

### P1 — adherence qualification, not self-attestation

Add probe/counter-probe fixtures for each load-bearing role instruction. Run them cold, with the
role contract, after same-session resume, and at the compaction boundary. Score deterministic
artifacts and broker requests first. A model echoing an instruction id is delivery evidence only;
it is never semantic qualification. Model/version changes invalidate qualification.

### P1 — closed advisory launch envelope

Launch the advisory client with an absolute executable, fixed argv, private home/config/temp
roots, a closed non-secret environment, and explicitly named credential sources. Bind executable,
argv, environment-policy, and non-secret config digests through external resume configuration and
the supervisor receipt. Keep opaque provider-side state as an explicit nonclaim.

### Implemented — disposition-bearing influence

The orchestrator remains advisory and holds no authority. Its leverage is the resident dispatcher:
an accepted structured finding blocks the next dispatch until the Validator records one of
`stop`, `narrow`, `escalate`, `refute`, or `resolve`, with a bounded reason, the exact pending-file
digest, and exact run-owned evidence bytes. Factory copies those bytes to a content-addressed
disposition artifact before it receipts or clears the subject. Merely reading the event cannot
release the gate. The orchestrator may narrow or stop the
next task through this attention gate; it may not rewrite a directive, amend a phase artifact,
grant a capability, or advance state.

Acquiring the crash-released role-dispatch mutex under the shared attention lock is the admission
linearization point. A blocking append before that acquisition is observed and refuses before
dispatch bytes are frozen; a blocking append after it applies to the next dispatch and cannot
interrupt or mutate the already admitted invocation. Process death releases the mutex, so exact
retained inputs remain retryable without an operator deleting a stale marker. An
unavailable Validator leaves the run blocked and emits an out-of-band human escalation;
elapsed time never fabricates a human disposition or silently proceeds. A Validator may refute an
untrusted advisory finding with evidence because the orchestrator has no judging authority; a
Critical directive or phase-intent change still takes the existing independent human gate.

## Schema, retention, and cutover

The exact instruction contract, readback, role contract, capsule, and prompt are retained under
the run evidence/control root and content-addressed by the capsule and receipt. They are not copied
into Kindex. Kindex stores only the durable architectural decision or a content digest. Explicit
resource-ledger classification and erasure execution for these new artifact classes remain a
retention follow-up; until then Factory claims durable local evidence, not independently enforced
erasure. Supersession never rewrites old evidence.

Pre-P0 runner receipts, state capsules, directive selections, and in-flight attempts are not
upgraded. Cutover creates a new profile digest and requires a new externally custodied checkpoint,
configuration set, and runner qualification. The first post-cutover run re-qualifies the small
deterministic seed; old runs remain historically honest and operationally non-resumable.

## Failure semantics

- Missing or invalid instruction state: refuse before a model call.
- Unresolved apparent conflict or ambiguous scope: block for a named human/Validator decision.
- Role contract absent, stale, or over budget: refuse before a model call.
- Model violates soft guidance: its result is rejected by objective evidence where a check exists;
  otherwise the gap remains visible and cannot be upgraded into a deterministic claim.
- Advisory client cannot be launched from the admitted envelope: retain `did-not-run`; do not
  synthesize advice or fall back to ambient configuration.
- Provider-hidden instruction or memory state: outside the proof; qualify behavior and retain the
  nonclaim.

## Verification

- Missing, corrupt, hash-tampered, expired, superseded, substituted, and oversized directive
  sources refuse before either client.
- A bare `interpretation_confirmed: true` substring refuses; wrong run, generation, role, digest,
  id set, and ambiguity all refuse.
- Lane capsule, retained role contract, effective-directive artifact, task prompt, and receipt bind
  identical bytes.
- Provider/user instruction files and ambient memory remain absent from qualified lanes.
- Poisoned `HOME`, `CODEX_HOME`, arbitrary environment values, and client config do not reach an
  advisory child; only named credentials do, and no secret value appears in evidence.
- A blocking orchestrator finding cannot be cleared without an exact-subject disposition and
  retained evidence whose bytes match the supplied digest.
- `make check-purity`, `make check-doctrine`, focused tests, `make ship`, `make test-isolation`,
  and `make test-tessera` remain green.
